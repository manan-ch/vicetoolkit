Tier 1 Master Vault 5-Star Police Heat

The Leonida National Depository

Heavily fortified underground vault situated in the heart of Downtown Vice City. Features biometric laser tripwires, pressurized nitrogen vaults, and rapid-response tactical SWAT garrisons.

Max Gross Take: $2,800,000
Recommended Approach: Subterfuge / Underground Drill
Recommended Roster: Elite Hacker + Heavy Gunman

Escape Vector: Storm drainage tunnels leading directly to the Vice City waterways via submersible watercraft.

Offshore Marine Score 4-Star Police Heat

Vice City South Beach Superyacht

Private luxury vessel anchored 2 nautical miles off South Beach. Contains un-cut diamonds, bearer bonds, and gold bullions stored inside the owner's master stateroom security lockbox.

Max Gross Take: $1,400,000
Recommended Approach: Night Water Infiltration
Recommended Roster: Tactical Infiltrator + Boat Pilot

Frequently Asked Questions: GTA 6 Heist Mechanics

Common technical queries regarding net payouts, crew cuts, laundering penalties, and police responses in Leonida.

The Leonida National Depository located in Downtown Vice City is the highest grossing robbery confirmed in GTA 6, featuring up to $2,800,000 in physical cash, gold bullion, and safety deposit valuables. Final net profit depends on the cuts negotiated with your hired hacker, gunman, and driver, as well as the standard 10% laundering fence fee.

Stolen bills from Leonida vaults are marked, ink-stained, or serialized. To spend your robbery proceeds on weapon armories, vehicle tuning, and safehouse properties, cash must pass through underworld laundering channels. A flat 10% fee is deducted directly from your gross loot before funds are deposited into your clean account balance.

Entry-level street robberies like the Vice Shores 24/7 Pawn & Liquors can be executed 100% solo with no crew members. However, Tier 1 and Tier 2 multi-stage operations (such as the First Trust Bank and The Depository) strictly require specialists: a Hacker to bypass digital locks before alarms trip, a Gunman for crowd suppression, and a Driver to position high-speed getaway vehicles.

Police heat ranges from 1 to 5 stars. At 1 to 2 stars, local VCPD squad cars patrol the immediate sector. At 3 stars, tactical road blockades and police helicopters deploy. At 4 to 5 stars, state-level SWAT Bearcat armored vehicles, sniper helicopters with thermal imaging, and spike strips are mobilized across major Vice City bridges.
