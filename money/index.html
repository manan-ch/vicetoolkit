Apollo Aggregates Warehouse: Infinite Cash & Contraband Loop

Discovered in South Vice-Dale County (East of Hamlet, North of Key Lento), this industrial aggregate facility holds high-tier narcotics packages and cash boxes that respawn infinitely via vehicle trunk caching.

Step 01 • Infiltration
Enter red warehouse back-office; collect 4 cash bricks & narcotics stash.
Step 02 • Trunk Cache
Walk to your parked vehicle and hold trunk interaction to lock loot in vehicle stash.
Step 03 • Character Swap
Swap between Jason and Lucia (or quick manual save/reload). Warehouse loot respawns instantly.
Step 04 • Fence Liquidation
Drive full vehicle trunk to Little Haiti black-market fence for clean cash conversion.
Tip: Keep a 1-star police evasion route through dirt canal backroads to avoid automated highway license-plate scanners.

Little Haiti Black-Market Fences

Standard pawn shops and merchant counters undervalue high-tier jewelry. Fencing luxury timepieces, uncut gemstones, and rare safe contents through designated Little Haiti syndicates yields triple baseline returns without triggering law enforcement suspicion.

Exotic Export $45,000 - $85,000

Port Gellhorn Chop Shop VIN Scramblers

Disabling GPS telemetry within 90 seconds of hotwiring luxury exotics (Torero XO, Emerus, Cheetah Classic) allows delivery to underground docks for instant non-traceable wire transfers. Delivering vehicles with zero body panels damaged grants a 15% pristine bonus.

Maritime Salvage $10,000 - $30,000

Keys Offshore Supply Drops

Maritime patrol vessels across the southern shelf can locate scheduled contraband airdrops marked by floating marine flares in shallow Keys waters during nocturnal weather cycles. Utilize a Seashark PWC or Dinghy equipped with scuba ballast for rapid retrieval.

Cargo Intercept $60,000 - $120,000

Commercial Dock Freight Hijacking

Container shipping manifests at the Port of Vice City schedule high-value electronics and luxury contraband convoys on Tuesday and Friday evenings. Ambush the lead escort with an EMP launcher to halt the convoy and secure the primary trailer.

Commercial Cashflow $12,500 / Day Passive

Ocean Drive Entertainment Laundering

Acquiring beachside hospitality venues unlocks weekly passive revenue deposits. Complete delivery restocking side missions to maintain club popularity at maximum 100% capacity, yielding consistent automated bank drops directly into the syndicate treasury.
